Calm Flight · CHS → CLT → LIT

A gentle, realistic practice trip. Start in the airport (security checkpoint, departures board, gate, jet bridge), then fly from a window seat: taxi, takeoff, climb through clouds, cruise, landing - by day, at sunset or at night. Fictional, unbranded airline.

Quest: press Enter VR (sitting is fine). Point a controller and pull the trigger for menu, prompts and glowing objects. In the airport: push the thumbstick forward to aim a teleport, release to jump (or press Continue for a guided walk). Thumbstick sideways = snap turn, grip = hide/show menu.

Desktop: drag to look, click menu/prompts/objects. Space pause · N skip · D day/sunset/night · T turbulence · S shade · B breathing · L lean to window · 1/2 leg · airport: ↑↓ walk, Enter continue
